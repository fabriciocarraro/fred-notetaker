Privacy Policy

Fred Notetaker · Last updated: July 9, 2026

Fred ("the app") is a self-hosted meeting notetaker operated by its administrator on private infrastructure. This policy describes what data Fred handles and how.

Information Fred accesses

  • Google account identity (name, email) — used to sign you in and to associate meetings with your account.
  • Google Calendar events (via the calendar.events scope) — Fred reads upcoming events to detect Google Meet meetings, and adds its own notetaker account as a guest on those events. Fred never creates, deletes, or otherwise edits your events, and does not read calendars you have not connected.
  • Meeting audio and metadata — when Fred joins a meeting as a visible participant, it records the meeting audio and captures participant names and speaking times in order to produce transcripts and notes.

How information is used

Calendar data is used solely to schedule Fred's attendance. Meeting audio is used solely to produce transcripts, speaker labels, summaries, and answers to your questions about the meeting. All processing — speech recognition, speaker identification, language identification, and summarization — is performed by open-source models running on the operator's own server. No meeting content, audio, calendar data, or Google user data is sent to third-party services, sold, or used for advertising or to train machine-learning models.

Google user data and Limited Use

Fred's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage and retention

  • All data is stored on the operator's self-hosted server.
  • Meeting audio recordings are deleted automatically after 15 days.
  • Transcripts, summaries, and notes are retained until deleted by you or the administrator.
  • Google OAuth tokens are stored encrypted at the operating-system level with restricted file permissions and are used only to poll your calendar.

Sharing

Meeting notes are visible only to the account that owns the meeting and to instance administrators. Access requires sign-in with Google and membership in an administrator-managed allowlist. Fred has no third-party integrations that receive your data.

Your controls

  • Disconnect your calendar at any time from the app's Settings page — this deletes the stored token immediately.
  • Revoke Fred's access entirely at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Request deletion of any meeting, transcript, or your account data by contacting the administrator.
